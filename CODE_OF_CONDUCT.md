# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project is committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for **everyone**, regardless of:
- Neurotype (neurodivergent, neurotypical, or any blend)
- Learning style or pace
- Age, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Disability status or accessibility needs
- Race, ethnicity, nationality, or caste
- Religion or lack thereof
- Socioeconomic status
- Education level
- Body type or appearance
- Geographic location

## Our Standards

### ✅ Encouraged Behaviors
- Use **welcoming and inclusive language**
- Be **respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences**
- Accept **constructive criticism** gracefully
- Focus on what's **best for the community**
- Show **empathy** towards other community members
- Ask for **clarification** rather than assume
- **Celebrate wins** together, no matter how small
- **Respect boundaries** — everyone needs different things
- **Accommodate neurodivergent needs** (extra time, written summaries, reduced sensory load)

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors
- **Harassment** in any form (sexist, racist, ableist, etc.)
- **Abuse, threats, or intimidation**
- **Discrimination** based on any identity
- **Unwelcome sexual or romantic advances**
- **Trolling, spam, or deliberately disrupting** conversations
- **Insults or personal attacks**
- **Sharing private information** without consent (doxxing)
- **Taking screenshots to mock or shame**
- **Gatekeeping** or gatekeeping language ("real coders don't...")
- **Demanding emotional labor** from marginalized folks
- **Repeating harmful tropes** about ADHD, autism, or other neurodivergences
- **Ableist language** ("that's so dumb," "crazy," "insane," etc.)

## Reporting

### If You Experience or Witness Harm

1. **If you feel safe,** you can directly address it with the person (some people genuinely don't realize)
2. **If you don't feel safe or it continues,** report it privately to:
   - Email: [maintainer contact — add to SECURITY.md]
   - GitHub: Use [private vulnerability report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories)

### What to Include
- Description of what happened
- Who was involved (if you're comfortable sharing)
- When/where it happened
- Any evidence (links, screenshots, witness info)
- What you'd like to happen next

### Our Response
- We'll acknowledge reports within **48 hours**
- We'll **keep your information confidential**
- We'll **investigate** and take action as appropriate
- We may ask follow-up questions to understand context
- We'll inform you of the outcome (without unnecessary details about the other party)

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub repositories under this project
- Issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments
- Discord/community chat (if applicable)
- Conferences, meetups, or events organized by or about this project
- Private communications if related to project participation

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
1. **Warning** with explanation of why the behavior wasn't okay
2. **Temporary block** from participating (24 hours - 7 days)
3. **Permanent removal** for serious/repeated violations

Maintainers have the right to:
- Delete comments violating this code
- Close issues/PRs used for harassment
- Block accounts engaging in abuse
- Report to GitHub Trust & Safety if needed

## Neurodivergent-Specific Guidance

### We Get It
- **ADHD**: Hyperfocus on certain discussions is normal; we may need gentle refocusing
- **Autism**: Literal interpretation, need for explicit instructions, preference for direct communication
- **Dyslexia**: Typos in comments/PRs don't matter; ideas do
- **General**: Different people need different things, and that's okay

### We Practice
- **Explicit communication** over hints and subtext
- **Patience** with different processing speeds
- **Accommodation requests** are welcomed, not burdensome
- **Quiet spaces** for those who need reduced stimulation
- **Clear, written summaries** of complex discussions
- **No pressure** to socialize beyond the work
